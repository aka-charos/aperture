# Getting Started: the Tour, the Checklist and Tips

Aperture shows you around the first time you sign in, keeps a short getting-started list on Home, and puts a one-line tip at the top of a screen the first time you open it. All three can be brought back from your settings.

## The Welcome Tour

The first time you sign in, a short tour starts by itself. It dims the screen and points at one thing at a time: your recommendations, rating, search, the assistant, the other places worth a visit, and where help lives. It only mentions the features your account has.

- **Next** and **Back** move through it; the arrow keys do too.
- **Skip tour** (or Esc) ends it at any point.
- **Read more** opens the guide page for that step beside the tour, and the tour waits until you close it.

If you used Aperture before the tour existed, it doesn't start by itself. A small card offers it once instead: **Take the tour** starts it, **No thanks** puts the offer away for good, and the × asks again the next time you sign in (the tips start showing in the meantime).

You can take the tour again whenever you like from your avatar menu (**Take the tour**).

## Show Me Around

Several screens have a tour of their own that walks through their controls: Home, Browse, a movie or series page, My Recommendations, Explore, and Settings → Recommendations. Open it from the **?** button in the top bar (**Show me around**, at the top of the guide drawer), or from the screen's first-visit tip.

## The Getting-Started List

A **Getting started** card at the top of [Home](dashboard.md) lists the things that teach Aperture about you:

| Item | Done when |
|------|-----------|
| **Watch something** | You've played a movie or an episode on your media server |
| **Rate 5 titles** | You've rated five titles (it counts as you go) |
| **Add an interest** | You've added an interest in [Settings → Recommendations](user-settings/recommendations.md) |
| **See why a title was picked** | You've opened a title that was scored for you and seen its panel |
| **Ask the assistant something** | You've started a conversation with the [assistant](ai-assistant.md) (only if you have it) |
| **Follow a show** | You follow a series in [Shows You Watch](shows-you-watch.md) (only if it's switched on) |

Each item ticks itself off, and clicking one takes you where it's done. Under the list, a line says whether your first recommendations are ready yet. A brand-new account has none until the next scheduled update has run, which is why My Recommendations can be empty on your first day.

The card goes away once everything is done, or when you close it with ×.

## First-Visit Tips

The first time you open some screens, a one-line tip sits at the top of the page, with **Show me around** (where the screen has a tour) and **Read more**. The × closes it for good. Tips wait until you've taken or skipped the welcome tour, or put off its offer.

## Small ? Icons

A few dense panels carry a small **?** beside their heading: the panel that explains why a title fits you, and the genre and weight cards in your settings. It opens the guide at the part that explains that panel.

## Bringing It All Back

**Settings → Display → Tour and tips** has two buttons:

- **Take the tour** starts the welcome tour again.
- **Show tips again** brings back the tips you've closed, and the getting-started list if you hid it.

Nothing about your recommendations changes when you use either.

## When an Admin Views the App as You

An administrator viewing the app as you sees your screens, but no tour or tip opens by itself, and nothing they do there is saved to your account.
