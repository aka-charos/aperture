# Collapsible Sidebar

The sidebar collapses to a slim icon rail when you want the content to take over the screen.

![Sidebar Expanded](../images/features/sidebar-expanded.png)

![Sidebar Collapsed](../images/features/sidebar-collapsed.png)

## Collapsing and Expanding

Two ways to toggle:

| Method | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **Logo click** | Click the app logo at the top of the sidebar |
| **Collapse button** | The **"Collapse sidebar"** button at the sidebar's bottom edge (becomes "Expand sidebar") |

## Collapsed Mode

The rail keeps icons for every section:

- **Hover (or focus) a rail icon** and a flyout slides out over the content with the full label and any sub-items — the layout beneath never moves
- Tooltips name each section
- Click to navigate as usual

## Remembering Your Choice

Your collapsed/expanded preference is **saved to your account** and follows you between browsers. Until you've ever toggled it, the initial state simply depends on window width — wide windows start expanded.

## Mobile

On small screens the sidebar becomes a **drawer** (the ☰ button in the app bar). The drawer always shows full labels — there's nothing to collapse.

## Where's the Admin Section?

Admin tools are **not** in the sidebar — they live in the **app bar beside your avatar**, visible only to admins. The sidebar is your content navigation.
