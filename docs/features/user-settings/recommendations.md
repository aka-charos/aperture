# Recommendations Settings

The **Recommendations** tab holds everything that decides what Aperture recommends to you: your taste, what is allowed onto your list, and some fine-tuning most people never need. Changes save as you make them and apply the next time your recommendations are made.

## Accessing

Click your **avatar** in the top bar and pick **Recommendations**. It's the first tab of your settings.

The AI-written description of your taste (your Watcher Identity) is read on the [Watch Stats](../watch-stats.md) page; this tab holds the settings underneath it.

---

## Your Taste

What your recommendations are built from: your watch history, plus anything you add or adjust here. Under the heading, a status line says when your movie and TV taste was last read from your history (or *Not scanned yet*).

### Rescan Watch History

**Rescan watch history** re-reads your history and finds the genres and franchises you watch. If you have already adjusted anything, it asks which way to go:

| Choice | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Add New Only** | Keep your adjustments and only add newly found genres and franchises |
| **Reset All** | Clear everything and recalculate from your current history |

Newly found items are marked **NEW** and listed first.

### Your Interests

Themes, styles or kinds of story you want more of (*"Time travel stories"*, *"Dark comedies"*). Type one and press Enter. One list covers movies and TV.

### Libraries That Count

Switch a library off to keep what you watch there (a kids' library, say) out of your taste. Only libraries your account can open are listed. Aperture's own generated libraries and system libraries are always left out.

### Genres

One list for movies and TV alike. The card summarises what you get **more of** and **less of**; **Adjust** opens a slider for each genre, from **Less** through **Normal** to **Boost** (0 to 2, with 1 as normal). A genre can be removed from the list entirely.

### Franchises

Series and collections you have watched several of. Each has a slider from **Avoid** through **Neutral** to **Boost**; boost one to see more of it, avoid one to see less, or remove it. If you have both movie and TV libraries, a **Movies / TV Series** switch inside the card picks which list you're looking at.

Two settings decide which franchises are listed at all: **Franchise size** (how many titles of it the library holds) and **Watched by you** (how many of them you've seen). Changes to these apply at the next rescan.

## Your Picks

What is allowed onto your list, and what comes with it.

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Suggest titles I have already watched** | Off (the default): you are only offered titles you haven't watched or started (5% progress counts as started). On: titles you've seen may come back, for example to rewatch |
| **Titles you rated low** | What happens to titles you rated 1–3: **Exclude Completely** (recommended) keeps them off your list, **Penalize But Allow** lets them through when they strongly match everything else. **Review** lists those titles so you can change a rating or clear it |
| **Explain each pick** | Whether each recommendation carries a *"Why Aperture picked this for you"* note. Your admin may decide this for you, in which case the card shows the setting in force with a reset option. Turning it on doesn't add notes to recommendations already made |

## Advanced

**Show advanced settings** opens two cards.

### Use My Own Weights

Off, your recommendations use the scoring weights your admin set. On, you set your own, separately for movies and TV:

| Weight | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Similarity** | How strongly to favour titles close to your taste. Usually the one to keep highest |
| **Genre Discovery** | Rewards a familiar anchor plus a genre you rarely watch: partly new, rather than as strange as possible |
| **Community Rating** | How much to favour highly rated titles (IMDb, plus Metacritic and Rotten Tomatoes critic scores for movies). This score rarely changes, so a high value makes your list repeat itself |
| **Diversity** | How hard to push for variety when your final list is picked |

Only the ratio between them matters, so they don't need to add up to 100%. Each slider shows the default when you've moved away from it, and **Reset to Defaults** puts them all back.

**Recent watch history limit** (10–200) only matters when your taste can't be built the usual way: a stand-in is then made from this many of your recent titles.

### Keep My Taste Up to Date

Your taste is re-read from your watch history on a schedule, from every 7 days to once a year, set separately for movies and TV. Switch it off to keep your taste exactly as it is, for example on a shared account where someone else's viewing would otherwise reshape it.

---

## Tips

- **Rescan first, adjust second.** Let the scan fill the lists, then move the sliders.
- **Use Add New Only after adjusting**, or a reset throws your changes away.
- **Change one weight at a time**, then look at your next recommendations before changing another.
