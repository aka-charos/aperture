# Display Settings

The **Display** tab holds the settings for how things look while you browse.

## Accessing

Click your **avatar** in the top bar and pick **Display**.

---

## Show Rating Badge on Library Posters

Turn it off if your library artwork already carries a rating (many custom covers do). Covers from Discover and other TMDb sources always show their rating in the corner whatever you choose. Until you choose, the server default applies; once you have, a **Reset to server default** button appears.

## Similarity Graph

Two switches for the related-content graphs on detail pages and in the full-screen graph (see [Related-Content Graphs](../similarity-graphs.md)):

| Setting | Effect | Use it when |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| **Hide Watched Content** | Titles you've seen don't appear in the graph | You browse related titles to find something *new* |
| **Full Franchise Mode** | Keeps whole franchises together (all 26 Bond films) instead of trimming them | You'd rather see every entry of a franchise than its best-known few |

---

## Elsewhere

- **Language** is set from the **flag in the top bar**, not here. It holds two choices: the **app language**, and the **recommendations language** AI-written text is written in (recommendation notes, your Watcher Identity, the assistant), which can follow the app language or differ from it. Your admin decides which languages are offered; the flag is hidden when there is nothing to choose.
- **Grid or list view, sort orders and filter presets** are set on each page and remembered for your account (see [Grid & List Views](../grid-list-views.md) and [Browse](../browse.md)).
