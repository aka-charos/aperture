# Watch Stats

The Watch Stats page turns your watch history into analytics: what you watch, when you watch it, and how it compares with the rest of the household.

![Watch Stats Page](../images/features/watch-stats.png)

## Accessing Watch Stats

Navigate to **Watch Stats** in the sidebar (insights icon).

---

## Metric Tiles

The top row summarizes your all-time viewing:

| Tile | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Movies** | Distinct movies you've played |
| **TV Series** | Distinct series you've played |
| **Episodes** | Episodes watched |
| **Watch Time** | Cumulative viewing time (e.g. "101d 9h") |
| **Total Plays** | Every play, including rewatches |
| **Favorites** | Titles you've favorited in your media server |
| **Rewatched** | Titles you've played more than once |
| **Busiest Day** | Your single biggest viewing day |

"Favorites" is the favorite/bookmark flag on your media server, not your star ratings.

---

## Click Anything to Drill In

Nearly every tile, chart, and rank row opens a **breakdown dialog** listing the actual titles behind the number — "Showing 20 of 214, most recent first" — including genres, decades, ratings, months, days, times of day, actors, directors, studios, networks, favorites, and rewatches.

- Rows for a person include a **Full filmography** link to their [person page](person-pages.md)
- Rows for a studio include an **All titles** link to its [studio page](studio-pages.md)

---

## Genres You Watch

A donut chart of the genres in your viewing, with a **Movies / TV** toggle (movies and series are the same question asked of two libraries, so they share one card). A legend beside the donut shows each genre's count and share; hover a segment or legend row for exact figures, and click either to drill in.

---

## Watching Activity

A stacked area chart of movies and episodes watched, month by month, **covering the last 12 months**. Hover for exact counts; click a month to see what you watched in it.

---

## When You Watch

A heatmap of your activity by day and hour — brighter cells are heavier viewing. The **Busiest Day** tile names your record-setting day. These are the only charts that exclude watches you dated roughly (see [About the numbers](#about-the-numbers)).

---

## Where Your Hours Go

A pie chart splitting your watch time between movies and TV. TV time is estimated from episode runtimes, so it's an approximation — but a useful one.

---

## Your Taste vs. the Crowd

How the crowd rates what you watch:

- **Avg community rating** — the average score critics/community give to the movies you watch
- **Rating distribution** — a histogram of those ratings, bucketed by half-point ("Rated 7.5")
- **Guilty pleasures** — genres you watch a lot that critics rate below your average

This is about the *community's* ratings of your picks — not how generously *you* rate.

---

## Movies by Decade

A horizontal bar chart of which eras your movies come from, from the 1930s to today.

---

## Most Rewatched

A poster grid of titles you've played more than once, each with a "×N plays" chip. Rewatching something is the strongest signal of enjoyment there is.

---

## Most Watched Actors & Directors

Your most-seen people, with profile photos and how many of their titles you've watched. Click an entry to drill into the specific titles, then follow **Full filmography** to their person page.

---

## Top Studios & Top Networks

Your most-watched production studios (movies) and TV networks (series), with logos. Networks are counted per series. Click through to see the titles, then jump to the studio's catalog page.

---

## Your Taste in Words

Alongside the charts, your [Watcher Identity](user-settings/watcher-identity.md) appears as taste cards for movies and series — plain-language sentences describing what kind of viewer the numbers say you are.

---

## About the Numbers

- **Coverage** — everything is all-time (from your first tracked play, which the header names), *except* the Watching Activity chart, which covers the last 12 months.
- **Roughly-dated watches** — if you rated a title and told Aperture roughly when you watched it, that watch counts in totals and rankings but is excluded from every chart with a time axis (activity, heatmap, busiest day). When this applies, the header says how many.
- **Hidden libraries** — libraries your admin excluded from recommendations are excluded here too.

---

## Using Your Stats

- **Know your taste** — genre and decade charts show what you gravitate toward; the guilty-pleasures chips show where you disagree with the critics.
- **Feed your recommendations** — your genre weights and franchise affinities in [Watcher Identity](user-settings/watcher-identity.md) are built from this history.
- **Find new content** — drill into an under-watched genre, or follow a favorite actor to their filmography and request what's missing.
