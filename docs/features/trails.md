# Trails

Trails (sidebar: **Trails**, route icon; or **Follow its threads** on any title's page) shows how the titles in your library are linked through the things they actually share: the people who made them, a franchise, a studio or network, an uncommon keyword. Every link has a name, and every link says how many titles in your library share it, so you can tell a rare connection from a common one at a glance.

It is a separate page from the [Media Graph](explore.md). The Media Graph draws titles that an AI model judges similar and labels the lines afterwards; Trails draws no line that is not a named, shared credit.

---

## How links are weighed

- **Rarer links count for more.** A cinematographer who shot two films in your library links them far more strongly than a studio that distributed two hundred. The count under each link ("Only these two in your library", "5 titles in your library") is that rarity, stated plainly.
- **Roles count too.** Directing both titles is a stronger link than appearing in both, and sharing a keyword is weaker still. A person is one link whatever their role, so an actor who later directed connects the film they starred in to the one they made.
- **Very common things are left out.** A keyword, studio or person on a large share of your library stops being a reason and becomes a category, so it links nothing here. Genres and countries are never links.
- **Only your library counts.** Links, counts and titles are limited to the libraries you can open.

## Connect two titles

Pick a **From** and a **To** title (or press **Surprise me** for two well-connected titles a few steps apart). Trails shows the shortest trail between them, read left to right: a title, the named thing it shares with the next one, that title, and so on. Under each link, **Also shared** lists anything else the two titles have in common.

Up to two **other trails** follow, each going through different titles from the first, so you see genuinely different routes rather than variations on one.

If no trail exists, the page says so, and says which title has no named links at all (usually one whose credits have not been filled in yet).

## Follow a thread

Pick a title to see every named thread leading out of it, rarest first: each person, franchise, company or keyword it shares with the rest of your library, with the titles that thread leads to (a person's titles are shown oldest first, like a filmography).

Press the **corner button** on any poster to follow that thread: that title becomes the new starting point, and the titles you followed stay above the threads as **your trail**. Click any earlier title in the trail to step back to it, or **Back to the start**.

**Connect to another title** takes the current title into *Connect two titles* as its starting point.

## In common

Add two or more titles (up to the limit the page states) to see what they share: the people, franchises, companies and keywords at least two of them carry. Links shared by more of the chosen titles come first, and rarer links first among those. The small posters beside each link show which of your titles carry it; hover one for the role.

If the titles share nothing, **Find a trail between the first two** takes you to *Connect two titles*.

## From a title's page

Every movie and series page has a **Follow its threads** button beside its other actions. It opens Trails in *Follow a thread*, starting from that title. Pressed from a title opened over another page, it closes the title's details and goes to Trails; pressed from a title opened over Trails itself, it keeps what you had picked in the other two modes.

Further down, below the cast and crew, a **Strongest links** card names the title's three strongest threads: who or what each is, what it is in this title ("Director here", "This title's franchise"), and how many other titles in your library share it. The link under the card (**See all 12 threads in Trails**, with the title's own count) opens the full list. A title with no named links shows no card, and its **Follow its threads** button goes once the page has checked (as the card's place scrolls into view); if Trails can't be reached, the card is simply left out.

## Everywhere on the page

- **Click a poster** to open the title's details over the page; closing them returns you where you were.
- Posters carry your **watched tick** or **episode count**, as everywhere else in the app.
- The page remembers what you picked in its address, so **Back** and a shared link both return to the same trail.

## Why a title has no trails

Trails reads the credits your server and the metadata enrichment provide: directors, writers, cinematographers, composers and editors (films), creators (series), the top-billed cast, TMDb franchises and keywords, studios and networks. A title missing those credits has nothing to link through. New titles appear on the page within the hour after they are synced.
