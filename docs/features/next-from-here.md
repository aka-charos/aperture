# Next from here

Next from here (sidebar: **Next from here**) answers one question: having watched a title, what should you watch next? It starts from your most recent title you finished and didn't rate badly, or from any title you pick, and gives two short lists. Every suggestion says why it is there, in terms you can check.

---

## Two lists

- **Close in feel**: the titles nearest the start in tone and subject, the same similarity the [Media Graph](explore.md) draws. A title here may also share people with the start, and when it does, that is the first reason it gives.
- **Same people, a different feel**: titles made by people from the start (its director, creator, writer, cinematographer, composer or lead actors) that do *not* feel like it. Sharing only a franchise is not enough to appear here. Closeness alone would never suggest them. No one person or franchise takes more than two places in this list, so it never turns into a single director's filmography.

Only titles you haven't finished, started or rated are suggested (a film counts as started once it is played a little way in), and only from the libraries you can open.

## The reasons

Each title shows up to three reasons, the most telling first:

- **Someone on both**: "Director of both: Hal Hartley", or, when the roles differ, "Clint Eastwood: Lead actor on *Dirty Harry*, Director here". Only roles that shape a title count. An editor, a bit part or a studio never links two titles here.
- **Someone you keep coming back to**: a person with a major role on the suggestion whose titles you have liked at least twice (see below for what counts). The reason says how many you liked and, when you rated some, what your ratings of their titles average (all of them, low ones included).
- **A network or studio you favour**: one whose titles you finish far more often than its share of your library would suggest ("you finish 3.2× as many of its shows as your library would suggest"). It is measured the same way as the networks in your [Watcher Identity](watch-stats.md). A studio or network that is simply everywhere in your library scores nothing, however much of it you watch.
- **Close in feel**: where it ranks among the films, or among the shows, closest to the start (#1 is the closest of its kind in your library, counting titles you have already seen). Films and shows are ranked apart because their distances are not comparable.

What counts as liking a title: rating it 7 or higher, or finishing it without rating it. A title rated 4 or lower counts against the people on it.

## Moving on

- **Start from** lets you pick any title in your library. **Recently watched** offers your latest finished titles as one-click starts.
- The **corner button** on a suggestion's poster makes that title the new start, so you can keep going from one suggestion to the next.
- **Click a poster** to open the title's details over the page.
- The start is kept in the page's address, so **Back** and a shared link return to the same list.

## Why a list is short or empty

- Close in feel needs the title's AI embedding. A title without one (new, or not yet embedded) gets only the people-based list.
- Someone you have watched a lot may have nothing left: suggestions leave out everything you have finished, started or rated.
- The credits come from your media server and the metadata enrichment. New titles appear here within the hour after they are synced.
