# Watch This

**Watch This** is where people you're connected to recommend titles to each other: what they sent you, and what you sent them and whether they watched it.

## Connections

A **connection** links your account with someone else's, both ways. Only an administrator can make one (on the Users page); you can't request or accept one yourself. Once you're connected to someone:

- **Watch This** appears in your sidebar once something has been sent to you or by you, with a badge counting what's waiting for you (the page itself is always at `/watch-this`)
- You can **send them titles** with **Recommend to…** on any movie or series page
- You see **their name** among the people who watched or favourited a title on its detail page, and they see yours
- You can read **their watch history** (read-only) from the picker on [Watch History](watch-history.md)
- The dashboard shows a **Recently watched by {name}** carousel for each of them
- If they turn out to be your **taste twin** (someone whose viewing overlaps yours far more than chance), your recommendation panel names them instead of saying "someone here"

Everything you see of a connection is limited to the libraries **you** can open yourself. A connection is hidden while either of you has no access to Aperture, and comes back with it.

---

## Sending a Recommendation

1. Open a movie or series and choose **Recommend to…** (next to Favorite)
2. Tick the people to send it to and press **Send**

The dialog marks anyone who can't receive it: **Not in their libraries** (they can't open the library it's in), **Already watched**, or **Already sent** (sending again moves it back to the top of their list).

---

## The Received Tab

Titles your connections sent you, grouped by who sent them, with **Movies** and **Series** separately. Each card says who recommended it. A title **leaves on its own once you finish it**; **Dismiss** removes it sooner. Whoever sent it isn't told you dismissed it.

On Emby, these titles can also appear as a **Recommended by friends** row on your home screen, if your admin has switched that row on (see [Emby Home Screen](user-settings/home-screen.md)).

## The Sent Tab

What you've recommended, grouped by person, each with a status:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Watched** | They finished it |
| **Watching** | A series they've started (e.g. "3/10 episodes") |
| **Waiting** | They haven't watched it yet |
| **Can't open** | It's no longer in a library they can open |

The tiles at the top count what's waiting for you, what you've shared, and how much of it they've finished.
