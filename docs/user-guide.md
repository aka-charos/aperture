# Aperture User Guide

Welcome to Aperture! Aperture is a recommendation and watch-tracking layer on top of your Emby or Jellyfin server. This guide covers all features available to users.

Depending on how your administrator configured the server, a few of the features below may be switched off or hidden (each one says so).

Inside Aperture, the **?** button in the top bar opens the pages that describe the screen you're on (and, on many screens, a **Show me around** tour of it), and **User guide** in your avatar menu opens this guide. **Take the tour** in the same menu shows you around the whole app.

## Personalizing Aperture

Your settings open from the avatar menu, in up to four tabs: Recommendations, Display, Account (when it has something on it) and Emby home screen (Emby only). Your language is set from the flag in the top bar.

29. [Recommendations](features/user-settings/recommendations.md) - Your taste (interests, genres, franchises, libraries), what may be recommended, and your own scoring weights
30. [Display](features/user-settings/display.md) - Poster rating badges and the similarity graph, plus where the language setting lives
31. [Account](features/user-settings/account.md) - Your email address, Trakt, and the legacy AI library names
32. [Emby Home Screen](features/user-settings/home-screen.md) - The rows Aperture adds to your Emby home screen, and where they go
